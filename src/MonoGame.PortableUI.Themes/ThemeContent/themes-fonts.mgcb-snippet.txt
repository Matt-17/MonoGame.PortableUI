Paste the blocks below into your game's Content.mgcb (after copying the
ThemeContent/Fonts files into your Content/Fonts folder). The names follow the
MonoGame.PortableUI FontManager convention: Fonts/{name}-{style}-{size}.

#begin Fonts\pressstart2p-regular-14.spritefont
/importer:FontDescriptionImporter
/processor:FontDescriptionProcessor
/processorParam:TextureFormat=Compressed
/build:Fonts\pressstart2p-regular-14.spritefont
